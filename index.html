Greenskeeper

Privacy Policy & App Support

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

Welcome to Greenskeeper. We are committed to protecting your privacy while helping you track and maintain your lawn care activities. This policy outlines how we handle data and how you can get support.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on your usage, Greenskeeper may collect the following information:

  • Camera and Photo Access: Requested solely to allow you to capture photos of your lawn, application logs, and garage inventory. Photos are processed and stored securely for your personal yard activity log and are never used for advertising.
  • User Inputs: Data you manually log, such as mowing dates, chemical and fertilizer applications, and garage inventory records.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Automated diagnostic data (such as crash logs, app startup times, and anonymous device identifiers) processed via Google Firebase to help us monitor performance and fix bugs.
  • Firebase Authentication: If you create an account, we securely collect your email address and authentication credentials to sync your data across devices.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly to provide the core functionalities of the app:

  • Storing and organizing your lawn care logs and inventory.
  • Syncing your data and photos across your personal devices.
  • Diagnosing crashes and improving the overall stability of the application.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We utilize Google Firebase for our backend infrastructure (database, authentication, and crash reporting). Data processed by Firebase is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Control & Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can manage or revoke Camera permissions at any time through your iOS device settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera > Greenskeeper).

If you wish to permanently delete your account and clear all stored lawn care data and photos from our database, please contact us using the support information below.
