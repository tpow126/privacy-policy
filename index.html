Privacy Policy for Greenskeeper Effective Date: September 30, 2026 Greenskeeper ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our application (the "App"). 1. Information We Collect Depending on how you interact with Greenskeeper, we may collect the following types of information: A. Camera and Photo Access Camera Access: Greenskeeper requests permission to access your device’s camera and photo library. Purpose: This access allows you to take and upload photos of your lawn, progress logs, or garage inventory. Storage: Photos captured using the camera are processed on your device and uploaded to our secure database to display within your app log. We do not use your photos for advertising, facial recognition, or tracking purposes. B. Data Collected via Firebase Services We use Google Firebase to provide backend functionality and improve app performance. Firebase may automatically collect certain data, including: Firebase Authentication: If you create an account, we collect your email address, unique user ID, and login credentials to manage your account and secure your data. Firebase Analytics & Performance Monitoring: May collect device identifiers (such as IP address or IDFV), app interaction data, startup times, and system events to help us analyze app performance and usage patterns. Firebase Crashlytics: Collects crash reports, stack traces, and device state information at the time of a crash to help us fix bugs and improve stability. C. User-Provided Information Information you manually enter into the App, such as lawn treatment records, mowing logs, chemical application amounts, and garage inventory entries. 2. How We Use Your Information We use the collected information solely to: Provide, maintain, and improve the functionality of Greenskeeper. Secure your account and authenticate your logins. Store your lawn care logs, inventory data, and uploaded photos so they sync across your devices. Monitor app performance, diagnose technical issues, and improve overall app reliability. 3. Third-Party Data Sharing We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share data with third-party service providers necessary to operate the App: Google Firebase: We use Firebase infrastructure for hosting, database management, authentication, and error reporting. Google processes this data in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy Terms. We may also disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities. 4. Data Retention and Security Data Retention: We retain your account information, lawn care logs, and inventory data for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account or request data deletion, we will purge your stored records from our Firebase database. Data Security: We implement industry-standard security measures provided by Google Firebase to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no electronic transmission or storage method is 100% secure. 5. Your Rights and Choices Camera Permissions: You can revoke or grant camera permissions at any time through your iOS device settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera > Greenskeeper). Account & Data Deletion: You may request the deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by contacting us at the email provided below. 6. Children's Privacy Greenskeeper is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." 8.
