Effective Date: September 30, 2026

Welcome to Greenskeeper. We are committed to protecting your privacy while helping you track and maintain your lawn care activities. This policy outlines how we handle data and how you can get support.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on your usage, Greenskeeper collects the following information:

  • Camera and Photo Access: Used solely to allow you to capture photos of your lawn, application logs, and garage inventory. Photos stay within your private account and are never shared or used for advertising.
  • User Inputs: Logs you manually create, including mowing dates, chemical and fertilizer application records, and garage inventory tallies.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Automated diagnostic data (such as crash logs, app startup times, and device IDs) processed via Google Firebase to help us improve stability and fix bugs.
  • Firebase Authentication: Email and user ID credentials used to secure your account and sync your data.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data exclusively to:

  • Provide core app features, including syncing your logs across devices.
  • Store photo entries for your personal yard activity log and inventory.
  • Diagnose technical crashes and monitor app performance.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data. We use Google Firebase infrastructure to manage backend services. For more information, review the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Control & Deletion

You can manage camera permissions anytime in your iOS Settings. If you wish to delete your account and clear all stored lawn care data, please contact us using the support email below.

Greenskeeper ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

A. Camera and Photo Library Access

  • Camera Access: Greenskeeper requests permission to access your device’s camera and photo library.
  • Purpose: This access allows you to take and attach photos to your lawn care logs, treatment records, or garage inventory.
  • Data Handling: Photos captured or selected are processed locally and uploaded to our secure database to display within your personal app account. We do not use your photos for advertising, marketing, facial recognition, or tracking purposes.

B. Data Collected via Firebase Services

We use Google Firebase services to provide essential backend infrastructure, authentication, and performance tracking. Firebase may collect certain data automatically, including:

  • Firebase Authentication: If you register an account, we collect your email address and unique user ID to maintain your account and secure your personal data.
  • Firebase Analytics & Performance Monitoring: Collects device identifiers (such as vendor ID / IDFV), IP addresses, app startup times, and user interaction statistics to help us optimize app functionality.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Collects crash logs, stack traces, and device state details when an error occurs to help us diagnose and repair technical issues.

C. User-Provided Lawn Data

  • Information you manually enter into the App, such as mowing schedules, chemical application entries (fertilizer, insecticide, herbicide), and garage product inventory lists.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the features of Greenskeeper.
  • Authenticate your identity and secure your app account.
  • Sync your lawn maintenance logs, garage inventory, and attached photos across your logged-in devices.
  • Diagnose bugs, monitor performance, and enhance the user experience.

3. Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share data with essential third-party service infrastructure:

  • Google Firebase: We utilize Firebase for database management, user authentication, and error monitoring. Data processed by Firebase is managed under the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy Terms.

4. Data Retention & Security

  • Retention: Your lawn logs, inventory records, and account details are retained for as long as your account remains active.
  • Deletion: You may request complete account and data deletion at any time by reaching out via our support email below.
  • Security: We utilize industry-standard encryption and security protocols provided by Google Firebase to safeguard your data.

5. Your Rights & Choices

  • Device Permissions: You can grant or revoke camera access at any time in your iOS system settings via Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera > Greenskeeper.
  • Account Deletion: Contact us to delete your account data permanently from our servers.
